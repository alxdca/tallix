# Yearly Budgets and Sharing

Use the Budget dropdown at the top of the sidebar to switch between top-level budgets, such as
**Family budget** or **Personal budget**. Use the Year dropdown below it to choose which year
inside that budget you want to view.

Each year still has its own budget, transaction, account and planning data. The top-level budget
groups those years for navigation and sharing.

## Create a budget

1. Open your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My account**.
2. Under **Create new budget**, enter a budget name, such as Family budget or Personal budget.
3. Enter the initial year for that budget.
4. Click **Create new budget**. Tallix selects the new budget and its initial year.

You can create more than one budget for the same year. For example, you can have a Family budget
for 2026 and a Personal budget for 2026.

## Add a year

1. Open your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My account**.
2. Select the budget you want to extend in the sidebar Budget dropdown.
3. Under **Add year to selected budget**, enter the year.
4. Click **Add year**.

Tallix copies category items from the latest preceding year in that budget, with planned
amounts reset to zero and no transactions copied. Opening account balances start from that
year's December closing balances as fixed snapshots; you can edit them independently.
If there is no preceding year, the year starts empty apart from savings account categories.
Existing transactions, planned values, account balances and asset values stay in their years.
Category groups are shared across the budget.

## Delete a budget

1. Open your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My account**.
2. In **My budgets**, click **Delete** next to the budget you want to remove.
3. Check the budget name and years in the confirmation, then click **Delete budget**.

Only the owner can delete a budget. Deletion permanently removes all years inside that budget,
including their categories, planned amounts, transactions, transfers, account balances, assets
and sharing access. Your accounts and other budgets are kept. Export a backup from
**Settings → Backup** first if you need a copy.

If you delete the selected budget, Tallix switches to another budget you own. If it was your
last owned budget, Tallix creates a new empty budget for the current year.

## Share a budget

1. Select the budget you want to share.
2. Open **Settings → Sharing**.
3. Enter the email address of an existing Tallix user and choose read-only or edit access.

Sharing applies to the selected top-level budget and all of its years. Other budgets keep their
own access settings. Select a different budget to manage its collaborators separately.

## Existing history

Existing separate yearly budgets remain separate budgets. Tallix does not implicitly merge them
by name or by year. Their transactions, categories, planned values and historical asset values
are retained. Existing collaborators keep their access to those existing budgets until you
change access for each budget independently.
