# Yearly Budgets and Sharing

Use the Budget dropdown at the top of the sidebar to switch between yearly budgets.
Past, current and future years use the same budget, transaction, account and planning views.

## Create a yearly budget

1. Open your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My account**.
2. Under **Create new budget**, enter the year and an optional description, such as Household or Travel.
3. Optionally select a budget you own from the previous year as the parent. Tallix copies its categories and links its December account balances to the new budget's starting balances. When the parent's balances change, linked starting balances update too. Planned amounts and transactions are not copied.
4. Click **Create new budget**. Tallix selects the new budget.

Each budget has its own categories and transactions. To stop linking an individual account's opening balance, edit that balance in the child budget. You can create more than one budget for the same year.

## Delete a yearly budget

1. Open your user menu at the bottom of the sidebar and choose **My account**.
2. In **My budgets**, click **Delete** next to the budget you want to remove.
3. Check the year and description in the confirmation, then click **Delete budget**.

Only the owner can delete a budget. Deletion permanently removes its categories, planned
amounts, transactions, transfers, account balances, assets and sharing access. Your accounts
and other budgets are kept. Export a backup from **Settings → Backup** first if you need a copy.

If you delete the selected budget, Tallix switches to another budget you own. If it was your
last owned budget, Tallix creates a new empty budget for the current year.
If a deleted budget was a parent, its children keep their latest effective opening balances as fixed values.

## Share a budget

1. Select the yearly budget you want to share.
2. Open **Settings → Sharing**.
3. Enter the email address of an existing Tallix user and choose read-only or edit access.

Sharing applies to the selected budget only. Other years and budgets keep their own access settings.
Select a different budget to manage its collaborators separately.

## Existing history

Existing budget years appear as separate entries in the dropdown. Their transactions,
categories, planned values and historical asset values are retained. Existing collaborators
keep their access to those years; you can then change access for each budget independently.
